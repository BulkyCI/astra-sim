## 1. The question

Does a retuned DCQCN close the gap between the fixed-low-tolerance
baseline and FORGIVE at 4:1? Pre-registered outcome that changes the
paper: a setting under which the baseline finishes within 5 points of
FORGIVE. Settings (provenance in `experiment-plan-2026-10-02.md`): rate
increase ×4 and ×¼ (`rate_ai_fraction`, with the hyper-increase scaled
alike); ECN thresholds ×2 (HPCC's linear rule at 400 Gbps, KMIN/KMAX
1600/6400 KB) and ×½ (400/1600 KB).

## 3. Reading

- **The kill condition did not fire.** The closest setting, HPCC's
  thresholds, leaves the baseline 12.9 to 13.5 % behind FORGIVE, not
  within 5 points. Across a sixteen-fold span of increase rate and a
  four-fold span of marking thresholds the gap is 12.9 to 18.2 %.
- **The increase rate does not matter.** ×4 and ×¼ move the baseline by
  less than half a percent and leave its notification count unchanged.
  On this collective the controller's cost is on the decrease side: how
  often it is told to cut, not how fast it climbs back.
- **Later marking helps the baseline, and FORGIVE keeps its share.** HPCC's
  thresholds halve the notifications and make the baseline 5 % faster, at
  the price of more trims (13.6 to 14.4 % against 12.0 to 12.9 % of
  data-parallel bytes repaired) and more retransmission (4.0 to 4.2 %
  against 3.5 to 3.7 %). The controller's total cost drops from 20 to
  16 %, and FORGIVE recovers 81 to 84 % of it, the same share as under the
  default. Earlier marking does the reverse: cost 23 to 24 %, FORGIVE
  recovers 73 to 77 %.
- **FORGIVE's absolute time barely moves** (1396 to 1468 ms across all
  five settings) because its data-parallel senders are exempt for 80 to
  85 % of flow time; the controller's parameters reach them only on the
  critical steps and after a budget is spent. The ×½ setting is where that
  residual exposure shows (1445 to 1468 ms).

## 4. What this changes in the paper

One sentence in the ablation paragraph or Limitations, replacing the
planned "no parameter sweep was run": the gap holds at 12.9 to 18.2 %
when DCQCN's additive increase is scaled by ¼ to 4 and its ECN thresholds
by ½ to 2 (the latter being HPCC's parameterization at 400 Gbps), and in
every setting FORGIVE recovers 73 to 88 % of the time the controller
costs. Framing per the agreed standard: the default is the simulator's
shipped parameterization, untuned in either direction; the sweep shows
the result is not an artefact of it. No figure.
