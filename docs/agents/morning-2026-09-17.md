# Morning summary, 2026-09-17: what to tell Yashar and Zechen

Joe Fang's work in collaboration with Zechen Ma. Every number below is
certified (the per-cell guarantee verified on every arm) and in
[results-ledger.md](results-ledger.md); the decks carry the same numbers.

## The headline

FORGIVE, on the worst cell of the map (64 ranks, `direct7`, 4:1
oversubscription, DCQCN), at budget 0.1, against DCQCN with no loss
tolerance:

| what | training time recovered | gradient bytes lost |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| forgive, obey DCQCN (no exemption) | 5.5 to 6.6 % | 6.8 to 7.0 % |
| FORGIVE v1, old rules (#123) | 12.9 to 14.1 % | 6.75 to 6.89 % |
| FORGIVE, design of record, no coin (#127) | 16.1 to 16.7 % | 7.55 to 7.57 % |
| FORGIVE with Yashar's coin, P = 0.25 (#124, #127) | 15.8 to 16.9 % | 5.5 to 5.9 % |
| the coin at P = 0.05 (#125, old rules) | 15.0 to 16.6 % | 1.2 to 1.3 % |
| no congestion control at all (#126) | 20.1 to 20.3 % | 0, but 25 % of bytes re-sent |

The sentence: the gain is how long a sender may ignore the congestion
controller; forgiveness is the currency that licenses it; the coin
spends that currency slowly enough that the licence lasts the whole
step for a loss inside the 0.7 to 3.3 % band MLT calls tolerable. DBLP's
own 40 % on GPT-2 is the tolerance evidence; nothing larger exists.

## What the references settled (#126)

- The fixed-low control is a true zero-tolerance baseline within the
  seed spread, so every delta stands as "against DCQCN with no loss
  tolerance".
- Forgiveness alone is about half the gain; the licence is the other
  half.
- No controller at all is the ceiling, 20 %, for 25 % of bytes re-sent;
  FORGIVE reaches 16 to 17 % for 5 to 7 % re-sent, and 19 % if it never
  re-engages (arm D).

## What the design of record measured (#127, last night)

- Vesting is the right budget law: the up-front cap gives 9 to 10 %
  against vesting's 16.1 to 16.7 %, because it spends the allowance early and
  the controller returns.
- The coin's value is loss, not time: at 0.25 it keeps the time and
  cuts loss from 7.6 to 5.5 %; at 0.05 (old rules) to 1.3 %.
- The stop spends every cell to its cap and recovers no time under
  selective repeat; it is not worth its loss here.
- Revocation costs about 2.3 points of time against never re-engaging
  and saves about a point of re-sent bytes; the exempt DP flows never
  slow the job's own tensor-parallel collectives.

## What is not measured

- Convergence at Llama scale; the tolerance rests on the GPT-2 runs.
- The coin below 0.25 under the design of record (measured only under
  the old rules).
- Uneven per-sender loss: no paper measures it inside an all-reduce; the
  pooled budget rests on the application rescaling from the forgiven
  ranges (written assumption, GPT-2 injection experiment to settle it).
- Budget 0.4, the DBLP-validated point, under the design of record.
